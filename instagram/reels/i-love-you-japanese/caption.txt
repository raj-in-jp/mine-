Nobody in Japan says "I love you" 🌙

愛してる (aishiteru) feels too heavy. Couples say 好き (suki) or 大好き (daisuki) instead.

And legend says Natsume Sōseki translated "I love you" as 月が綺麗ですね: "The moon is beautiful, isn't it?"

Send this to the one you'd say it to 🌙
