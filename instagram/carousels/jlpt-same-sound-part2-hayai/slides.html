早い
early · soon
about TIME
朝あさ早はやく起おきる。
I wake up early in the morning.
まだ寝ねるには早はやい。
It's still too early to sleep.
🔑 日 (sun) on top → time · 早朝

⏰ when it happens
→

→
速い
Fun fact: both have the same opposite → 遅おそい
