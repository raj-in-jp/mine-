1: JLPT carousel part 2: はやい has 2 kanji, 早い and 速い. Same sound, different kanji.
2: Sample JLPT question: 新幹線はとてもはやい. Choose 早い or 速い.
3: 早い means early, about time. Examples: 朝早く起きる, まだ寝るには早い.
4: 速い means fast, about speed. Examples: 新幹線はとても速い, 彼は走るのが速い.
5: Trick: when it happens is 早い, how fast it moves is 速い. Both opposites are 遅い.
6: Bonus: 早口 means talking fast and is written with 早. Example: 先生は早口だ.
7: Quiz: three sentences, pick 早 or 速 for each one.
