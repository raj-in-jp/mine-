1: JLPT carousel: あう has 3 kanji, 会う 合う 遭う. Same sound, different kanji.
2: Sample JLPT question: 駅で友だちにあう. Choose the right kanji from 会う, 合う, 遭う.
3: 会う means meet a person. Examples: 駅で友だちに会う, 明日先生に会います.
4: 合う means fit or match. Examples: 靴のサイズが合わない, 答えが合っている.
5: 遭う means run into something bad. Examples: 事故に遭った, 地震に遭った.
6: Trick: meeting a person is 会う, something fits is 合う, something bad is 遭う.
7: Bonus: 逢う, a romantic or fated meeting, used in songs like また逢う日まで.
8: Quiz: three sentences, pick 会, 合 or 遭 for each one.
