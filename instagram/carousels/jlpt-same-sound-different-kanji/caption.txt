あう has 3 kanji. Pick the wrong one on the JLPT and the point is gone 👀

会う: meeting a person
合う: something fits or matches
遭う: running into something bad, like an accident

The trick: ask what you're meeting. A person, a fit, or trouble.

Bonus on slide 7: 逢う, the romantic one you hear in Japanese songs.

If you're learning kanji for the JLPT, same-reading kanji like these show up in the vocabulary section, so they're worth 30 seconds now.
