Same sound, different kanji. The JLPT loves this trap 👀

あう, はやい, あつい, はかる, とる, かえる. Each one has 2 or 3 kanji, and the test gives you the word in hiragana and asks you to pick.

The trick: ask what it's about. A person? Time? Speed? Weight? The kanji follows.
