あう has 3 kanji, and the JLPT will make you pick 👀

会う: you meet a person
合う: something fits or matches
遭う: you run into something bad

The trick: ask what you're meeting. Person, fit, or trouble.
Bonus on slide 7: the romantic 逢う you see in songs.
